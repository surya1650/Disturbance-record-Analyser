# Noisy DSP and timestamp recovery implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans task-by-task, with one fresh whole-branch reviewer.

**Goal:** Validate noisy raw waveform DSP and explicit local timestamp recovery, fixing only reproduced defects.
**Architecture:** Independent records -> COMTRADE parser -> actual DSP -> guarded main/reviewed stages; separate provenance-aware recovery validator.
**Tech Stack:** Existing Python>=3.10, NumPy and pytest. No new dependencies.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-noisy-dsp-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No new field data or clock synchronization requirement; E5 remains unsynchronised.
- Preserve operational gates, previous oracles/artifacts, Dash, workbook, app database and preview.
- Keep every failure/refusal in its stratum and no fabricated confidence/field claims.
- Timestamp tolerance is a disclosed software policy, not a standard tolerance.

## Review Focus

- Missing, invalid, reset/gapped timestamps or bad sample numbers cannot gain trusted acquisition by fitting.
- Large constant timestamp origins and nonunity timemult must not lose local-time accuracy.
- Metadata-only clock skew cannot alter E5 results, promote E4 or mutate source timestamps.
- Weak/balanced waveform noise must remain nonzero and controls separate from accuracy denominators.
- Exceptions/failed numerical cases and exclusive-output races cannot erase planned observations or publish empty evidence.
